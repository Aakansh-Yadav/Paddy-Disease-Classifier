About

Paddy Doctor looks at a close-up photograph of a rice leaf and says whether it looks healthy or which problem it most resembles. It is decision support for field scouting. It does not replace a plant pathologist.

The check has two stages. Stage A decides healthy or unhealthy. Stage B names the disease only when the leaf looks unhealthy.

The ten labels are:

  • Healthy leaf
  • Bacterial leaf blight
  • Bacterial leaf streak
  • Bacterial panicle blight
  • Rice blast
  • Brown spot
  • Dead heart
  • Downy mildew
  • Rice hispa
  • Rice tungro

The photos it learned from are close-ups in the Paddy Doctor rice-leaf collection. A wide view of a field is outside that set.

Help

  1. On the first page, enter a username and a 10-digit mobile number. Email is optional. Continue opens the classifier.
  2. Choose or drop a JPEG or PNG close-up. One to three rice leaves, with any spots in the frame, in even daylight, works best.
  3. Click Classify. The diagnosis shows Stage A first. If the leaf looks unhealthy, Stage B names the closest disease and lists what to look for.
  4. A warning means the photo does not look like a training close-up. Wide field shots, sky, and golden ripe panicles are poor matches. A healthy call means a green leaf with no spots.
  5. Photos larger than 12 MB are rejected. Log out on the classifier to return to the login page.
